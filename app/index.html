Read this first — the only missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude

Meditate at every spot across Leonida — 18 spots in 10 locations, with Jason and Lucia. One spot can disappear forever: the first meditation, at Jason's home in Chapter 1. It is not yet confirmed whether the trophy requires that spot, so do it to be safe.

  1. After the mission “Down to Earth” (Chapter 1), go home and talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning.
  2. She says “You know what I'm gonna do? Meditate” — follow her out to the balcony.
  3. Choose “Accept” when the meditation prompt appears.
  4. Later spots need Jason and Lucia together, which unlocks after Chapter 2, “Coffee Break”.

Save strategy: GTA 6 gives you 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. Save at the start and the end of every chapter (10 saves) and keep the other 5 for free roam. A save from before the end of Chapter 1 is your insurance for this trophy.

Roadmap to the platinum

Three steps, in order. The story first, then the long checklist grind, then the strangers and the leftovers.

1

Story + optional missions

Play all 5 chapters and take the optional missions as they appear. Scan vehicles and study animals from the start — that progress counts later. Do not skip the Chapter 1 meditation (see the warning above). Save at the start and end of each chapter.

60–80 hours
2

The 100% checklist + 70 of 80 challenges

After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work the 35 items in the checklist below. Most categories only need a share of their total — 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 70 of 80 challenges. This is the longest step by far.

~100 hours
3

Strangers & Freaks + cleanup

Finish the 8 stranger strands and mop up the remaining one-off trophies — arcade games, the tank, the plane, the bowling alley and the rest. Everything stays available after the story, so nothing here can be missed.

~10 hours

Questions, answered

How many trophies does GTA 6 have?

55 on PlayStation 5: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze. On Xbox Series X|S the same 55 achievements are worth 1,000 Gamerscore, plus a 0G Mythic achievement in place of the platinum.

How long does the platinum take?

About 200 hours at an estimated difficulty of 4 / 10. The story is 60–80 hours; the 100% checklist is roughly 100 more. No difficulty setting affects any trophy, and there is no mission replay or New Game+ — free roam after the story is where the cleanup happens.

Are there any online trophies?

No. All 55 trophies are single-player and 0 are online or DLC at launch. That is the biggest difference from GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2, which both had 18 online trophies each.

Can I miss a trophy?

Only possibly one: Positive Mental Attitude, if the Chapter 1 meditation spot turns out to be required. Do it in Chapter 1 (see the warning at the top of this guide) and nothing else can be missed — every other trophy stays available after the story.

Do cheats disable trophies?

Yes. Using one of the game's 61 cheats disables trophies and saving for that session, and no cheat is even available before the credits. Save first, then cheat for fun after the platinum.

Is this the official trophy list?

Rockstar has not published an official list yet. The 55 names, descriptions and grades here are reported from PSN/Xbox data (Exophase, PSNProfiles) and guide sites (PowerPyx, GTABase), which agree on every entry. Five trophy triggers are still unknown to anyone and are marked “Trigger unconfirmed” in the list — this guide will be updated as the community cracks them.
